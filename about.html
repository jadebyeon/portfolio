About Me

Hi, I'm Jade Byeon.I design at the intersection of evidence and expression, believing that motivation is a measurable design outcome. With a dual background in Art & Design and Statistics, I translate complex data into emotionally engaging, high-fidelity experiences—owning the full cycle from research and strategy to robust, gamified systems that build lasting habits.

I was born in Korea and have studied in China, Singapore, and the United States, which shapes my sensitivity to diverse users and contexts. I’m currently exploring how persuasive technology and physical-digital systems can be designed ethically for better human–technology interaction.

When I’m not designing You’ll find me watching films, listening to music, or reading.
